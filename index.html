Sources

Every chart here is transcribed from the manufacturer's own tuning manual where one is published, or from settings guides that agree with it.

Settings charts describe the hosel, not the head: two clubs in the same family can share a chart but have different stated lofts and lie angles. Always check the numbers stamped on your own head, and remember a loft change also changes face angle and lie.
